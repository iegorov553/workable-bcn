WORKABLE BCN

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

This policy explains how the Workable BCN café map app handles your information. Workable BCN is an independent guide to cafés in Barcelona and nearby towns.

Support and privacy contact
For questions or requests about Workable BCN and your data, email workable.bcn@iegorov.com.

1. Location

The app asks for location permission when you use a location button on the map or in a list. If you already allowed access, the app obtains your current location at startup to show distances to cafés. Location buttons let you refresh distances or centre the map. Your coordinates are used in device memory. The app does not store a location history or track you in the background.

You can decline or revoke location permission in your device settings. Browsing cafés, searching and saving places remain available. Map requests made after centring on your location may reveal that area to the map provider, as described below.

2. Saved places

The identifiers of your saved cafés are stored locally on your device. Workable BCN does not sync them to an account or its own server. Android app backup is disabled. Remove an individual saved place using its bookmark button, or clear the app's local data to remove all saved places. In the web version, you can clear the site's storage in your browser.

3. Map tiles and external links

The map downloads map tiles over HTTPS from OpenStreetMap. The provider receives your IP address, technical request information and the map area requested. When you centre the map on yourself, this area may correspond to your location. The provider's handling and retention of these requests are covered by the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

When you request directions, the app opens Google Maps with the selected café's destination. Workable BCN does not add your own coordinates to that link. Google Maps may separately use or request your location under the Google privacy policy. Café source links open the relevant chain's website, which has its own privacy practices.

4. Accounts, advertising and analytics

Workable BCN has no user accounts, advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not operate a server to collect your in-app location, searches or saved places, and does not sell your personal information.

5. Support email

If you contact support, your email address and the information you choose to include are processed to respond to your request. Email is handled by the email service providers involved in delivering it. Please include only information needed for your request. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask about access, correction or deletion using the contact above.

6. Retention, deletion and security

Current coordinates stay in memory for the app session. Saved places remain in local storage until you remove them or clear that storage. Workable BCN cannot directly delete the map provider's request logs; contact that provider for requests about its data. You may contact support with questions about your privacy rights or local app data.

Network requests made by the app to the map provider use HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. This policy page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including your IP address, when you visit it. See the GitHub privacy statement. This page does not add analytics, tracking scripts or forms.

8. Changes

This page and its update date will change if the app's data practices change. The app will request any additional permission where required.
